# Welcome to the Coffee House!

This site serves primarily as a central hub for all of hobby work. As I develop an interest in various topics, I tend to create minimum viable products that help me understand the topic a little better. This site is where I post my findings.

If I'm dedicated enough to any singular project, I may post a link to a usable version (though this is rare). I'm also always exploring new ways to publish my thoughts so the site itself is a bit of an experiment and may be unstable from time to time (🤷 it's better to break this production site than a production app at work!)

That said, you can find some helpful links in the menu bar. By far, the Blog will contain the majority of the content. Please also stop by my Mastodon profile to let me know that you've visited my site. While you're there, ask me a question, send me a meme, or just say "Hi". I'm always excited to expand my circle of tech friends!
