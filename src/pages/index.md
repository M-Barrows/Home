# Welcome to the Coffee House!

This site serves primarily as a central hub for all of my hobby work. As I develop an interest in various topics, I create mini projects to cement my understanding. Sometimes these are tech topics like DevOps pipelines or game development. But sometimes, they can be things like TTRPG roll statistics. If i discover anything particularly interesting, I may make a blog post about it.

If I'm dedicated enough to any singular project, I may post a link to a usable version (though this is rare). I'm also always exploring new ways to publish my thoughts so the site itself is a bit of an experiment and may be unstable from time to time (🤷 it's better to break this production site than a production app at work!). 

That said, you can find some helpful links in the menu bar. By far, the `blog.html` will contain the majority of the content. Please also stop by my [Mastodon](https://hachyderm.io/@CodeAndCoffee) profile to let me know that you've visited my site. While you're there, ask me a question, send me a meme, or just say "Hi". I'm always excited to expand my circle of tech friends!
